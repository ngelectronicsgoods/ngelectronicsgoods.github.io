Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How does the activation key work?
Download the software and start a free 7-day trial. When you're ready to buy, open the app, click "Activate License", fill in your details, and click "Request via WhatsApp". Send the message, pay via UPI, and you'll receive an Activation Key on WhatsApp. Paste the key in the app and click Activate.
Is my data safe? Do photos go online?
100% safe. All face recognition runs offline on your computer. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. No internet connection is required after installation.
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) and Bank Transfer. After payment, send the screenshot on WhatsApp to receive your activation key.
How many photos can it scan?
Unlimited. Scanning speed depends on your PC - typically 5-50 photos per second depending on your hardware and photo resolution.
Can I use it on multiple computers?
One license is tied to one computer (Machine ID). Contact us for multi-PC licensing discounts.
What if SmartScreen blocks the app?
Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not yet code-signed. Click "More info" then "Run anyway". This is normal for independent software.
What is the refund policy?
Since this is digital software, we offer refunds only if the key doesn't work and we cannot fix the issue within 48 hours.
